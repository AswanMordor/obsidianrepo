
## Excersise List
- Bulgarian Split-Squats
- Glute Hip-Raises (barbell or smith machine)
- Seated Hamstring Curls
- Squats
- Romainian/Stiff Leg Deadlifts
- Cable Kick-Backs:
- Machine Chest Press
- Machine Shoulder Press
- Machine Pull Down (plate-loaded) OR Pull Up
- Face-Pulls

## Day A
1. Squats: 2 warmup sets, 5 sets of 3-5 reps with a min. 2 minute rest between sets
2. Romainian Deadlifts: 1 min back mobility, 1 warmup set, 3 sets 8-10 reps
3. Lunges (dumbbell or barbell): 1 warmup set, 3 sets of 10-12 reps per leg
4. Machine Chest Press: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
5. Face-Pulls: 3 sets of 10-12 reps

## Day B
1. Bulgarian Split Squats: 1 warmup set, 5 sets of 8-10 reps
2. Glute-Hip Raises (using barbell or smith machine): 5 sets 8-10 reps
3. Cable Kick-backs: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
4. Seated Hamstring Curls: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
5. Machine Shoulder Press: 5 sets of 8-10 reps
6. Machine Pull Down (plate-loaded): 3 sets of 8-12 reps OR pull-ups 3 sets to failure
